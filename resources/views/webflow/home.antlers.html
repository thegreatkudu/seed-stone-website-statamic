Talk to an engineer
Is HMXTECH a registered company?

Yes. HMXTECH Company Limited was incorporated in Tanzania in 2020 and is registered with BRELA (No. 478140), the Engineers Registration Board (No. 238L) and the Contractors Registration Board as a building contractor (B5/1393/07/2021) and civil works contractor (C6/2288/07/2021).

What kind of projects do you take on?

Buildings, from houses and apartments to offices, factories, warehouses and godowns; structures such as bridges, culverts, towers, dams and tanks; and civil works including roads, water supply and sanitation, drainage and irrigation.

Can you design and build, or only one of the two?

Both. As a registered consulting firm and contractor we can design and supervise a project for you, build to another engineer’s design, or take the whole project on a design and build contract.

How do you price a project?

Every project is priced from its drawings and bill of quantities. We take part in open competitive tendering, selected tendering, design and build tendering, the force account method and labour based technology.

Where do you work?

We are based in Mbeya (P.O. Box 1897) and carry out projects across Tanzania and beyond its borders.

Is the Truss Analyzer really free?

Yes. The Truss Analyzer web app is free to use in any browser, with a Windows version too. It follows the Northing-Easting method and checks indeterminate trusses with the stiffness method.
