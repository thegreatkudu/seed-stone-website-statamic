Talk to our team
How long does it take to go live?

Most clinics are live in 30 days. We connect your EHR, import your schedule and train the front desk in two short sessions.

Which EHRs do you integrate with?

Cobalt, Summit, Ledgerline, Clearpath, Oakfield and more, over FHIR and HL7. If yours is not on the list, we will check it on your call.

Is Carefront HIPAA compliant?

Yes. Every plan includes a signed BAA, encryption at rest and in transit, and a full audit log. We are SOC 2 Type II and HITRUST r2 certified.

Can patients pay through Carefront?

Yes. Patients pay copays and balances by card from a text link or at check-in, and payment plans split larger balances over 3, 6 or 12 months.

Do you charge per patient?

No. Plans are priced per provider. Patients, front desk staff, nurses and admins are always free.

Can we bring our existing intake forms?

Yes. Send us your current forms and we rebuild them as digital intake during onboarding, mapped to fields in the record.
