FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Call +255 758 526 792 or email info@hmxtech.co.tz.

Talk to an engineer
How do you price a project?

Every quote is built from your drawings and bill of quantities. If you have no drawings yet, we start with a site visit and a feasibility estimate, then quote the design.

What do you need from us for a quote?

The site location, what you want to build, and any drawings, surveys or title documents you already have. A phone call or a visit to our Mbeya office is enough to start.

Can you work on public and donor funded projects?

Yes. We take part in open and selected tendering, and support projects delivered under the Force Account Method and labour based technology.

Do you offer supervision only?

Yes. As ERB registered consulting engineers (238L) we can supervise works built by another contractor, check quality and certify valuations.

Do you work outside Mbeya?

Yes. We are based in Mbeya and work across Tanzania and beyond its borders.

How do you control quality on site?

Soil, concrete and steel are tested before we rely on them, and every stage is checked by a registered engineer before work moves on.

Tell us what you want to build.

Share your site and drawings. A registered engineer will reply with next steps and a quote.
