Our story

Design and construction under one roof.

HMXTECH was incorporated in Mbeya in 2020 (BRELA No. 478140) by engineers who wanted the people who design a project and the people who build it to work as one team.

We are registered with the Engineers Registration Board as consulting engineers, and with the Contractors Registration Board for both building and civil works. That lets us take a project from feasibility and design through construction to handover, and share what we learn through our publications and the free Truss Analyzer.
