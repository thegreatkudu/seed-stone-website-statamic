Instructions

How to make Carefront yours.

Everything is built natively in Webflow. Here is where colors, type, content, components and forms live, and how to change them in a few clicks.

Colors and modes

Every color is a variable in the Carefront color collection. Change a value once in the Variables panel and it updates everywhere. To recolor a whole page, select the Page wrapper and switch its variable mode to Blue, Forest or Plum.

Type and spacing

Fonts, heading sizes and spacing live in the Carefront type collection. Swap Font/Serif or Font/Sans to change the typeface across the site, then fine tune the Size and Space variables.

Research CMS

Posts live in the Research posts collection. Add a post with a header image, summary and body. Turn on Featured to show it in the large card at the top of the Research page.

Components

Nav, Footer and Call To Action are components. Edit one and the change applies on every page that uses it. Illustrations are inline SVG, recolored with the ill classes in the Style panel.

Interactions, forms and code

The hero and the three cards below it animate with Webflow Interactions; open the Interactions panel to change or remove them. All forms are native Webflow forms. The only custom code is the Webflow Way Validator snippet in Site settings, which you can delete after purchase.
