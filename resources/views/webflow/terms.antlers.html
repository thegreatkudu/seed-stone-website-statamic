Terms of service.

The agreement between Carefront and the clinics that use it. Last updated November 2026. This is sample copy for the template, so replace it with terms your counsel has reviewed.

Using Carefront

These terms apply to every clinic that signs up for Carefront and to the staff it invites. By creating an account, the clinic agrees to them on behalf of its team.

Accounts and access

Each person gets their own login. You are responsible for who you invite, the roles you assign and for removing access when someone leaves.

Your data

Clinics own their data and their patients' records. You grant us a limited right to process that data only to provide and support the service.

Business associate agreement

No protected health information may be added before a BAA is signed. The BAA forms part of these terms and wins if the two ever conflict.

Fees and billing

Plans are billed per provider, monthly or yearly, in advance. Prices are listed on the pricing page and change only with 30 days' written notice.

Availability

We target 99.9% monthly uptime and post planned maintenance at least 48 hours ahead. Service credits for missed targets are described in your order form.

Ending the agreement

You can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the paid period. We may suspend accounts that put patient data or other clinics at risk.

Liability

Carefront is provided as described in your plan. Each side's total liability is limited to the fees paid in the 12 months before a claim, except where the law does not allow it.

Changes to these terms

We will email clinic admins at least 30 days before any material change takes effect.
