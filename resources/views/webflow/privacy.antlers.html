Privacy policy.

How HMXTECH Company Limited collects, uses and protects the personal data you share through this website. Last updated November 2026.

Who we are

HMXTECH Company Limited (BRELA No. 478140) is an engineering consultancy and construction company based in Mbeya, Tanzania. We are responsible for the personal data collected through this website.

You can reach us at P.O. Box 1897, Mbeya, Tanzania, on +255 758 526 792 or +255 767 307 685, or by email at info@hmxtech.co.tz.

What we collect

When you request a quote: your name, email address, phone number, the type of project, the site location and anything you choose to tell us about the project.

When you sign up for news: your email address and the page you signed up from.

When the client portal opens: the account details you give us and the project information shared with your project team.

When you visit the site: standard server logs such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, used to keep the site secure and working.

How we use it

To answer your enquiry, visit your site if you ask us to, and prepare a quote or proposal.

To send you news and new publications, only if you ticked the box or signed up for them.

To keep the website secure and to fix problems.

We never sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

The law we follow

We handle personal data in line with Tanzania’s Personal Data Protection Act, 2022. We collect only what we need, use it for the purpose you gave it to us, and keep it accurate and secure.

Cookies

This site uses only the cookies it needs to work: a session cookie (hmxtech-session) and a security token (XSRF-TOKEN) that protects our forms from misuse. Both expire two hours after your last visit to the site.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

Who we share it with

Your enquiry is stored on our web server and sent to our company mailbox. The companies that host the website and our email can process it on our behalf, and only for that purpose.

Part of the page code is loaded from a content delivery network, which receives your IP address when your browser requests it.

We share personal data with authorities only when the law requires us to.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we need them to answer you and to keep a record of quotes and projects. We keep news sign-ups until you ask us to remove you.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or withdraw your consent to news emails at any time. Email info@hmxtech.co.tz and we will respond as soon as we can.

If you are not happy with how we handled your data, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission of Tanzania.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the way we handle personal data changes, and change the date at the top.
