Legal

Privacy policy.

How we collect, use and protect clinic and patient data. Last updated November 2026. This is sample copy for the template, so replace it with a policy your counsel has reviewed.

What we collect

Account details for clinic staff: name, work email, role and the location they work at.

Clinic data you add to Carefront: schedules, intake forms, messages, claims and the patient records they belong to.

Usage data from the app, such as pages viewed, device type and error logs, used to keep the service running.

How we use it

We use clinic and patient data only to run the service you signed up for: booking visits, syncing your EHR, sending reminders and submitting claims.

We never sell data, and we never use patient records to train models or target advertising.

Patient health information

Patient records are protected health information under HIPAA. We act as your business associate and sign a BAA with every clinic before any patient data enters the platform.

Staff only see the records their role allows, and every view and edit is written to an audit log.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher and at rest with AES-256. Production systems run in US data centers with daily encrypted backups.

We are audited annually against SOC 2 Type II, run third-party penetration tests twice a year and require single sign-on and hardware keys for our own team.

Sharing and subprocessors

We share data only with the subprocessors that host and operate Carefront, each bound by a BAA or equivalent agreement. The current list is available on request.

We disclose information to authorities only when the law requires it, and we tell the affected clinic unless we are prohibited from doing so.

Retention and deletion

Clinic data is kept for as long as your account is active. When you close an account, we export your records on request and delete them within 30 days, except where the law requires a longer retention period.

Your choices

Clinic admins can export, correct or delete records at any time from settings. Patients can request access to their own record through their clinic.

Questions

Send privacy questions through the contact page and a member of the team will reply within two business days.
