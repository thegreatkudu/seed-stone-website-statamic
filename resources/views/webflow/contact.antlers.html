FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can’t find what you need? Call +255 758 526 792 or email us.

Email info@hmxtech.co.tz
Where is your office?

We are in Mbeya, Tanzania, at P.O. Box 1897. Call ahead and we will arrange to meet you at the office or on your site.

Do you visit the site before quoting?

For most building and civil works, yes. A firm quote depends on ground conditions, access and the drawings, so an engineer will usually see the site first.

Can I send drawings with my enquiry?

Yes. Email drawings, surveys or a bill of quantities to info@hmxtech.co.tz, or mention them in the form and we will ask for them.

Do you take on small projects?

Yes. We work for individual clients as well as public and private institutions, from a single house to roads and public buildings.

Is the call or first meeting free?

Talking through your project costs nothing. If a site visit, survey or testing is needed before a quote, we will tell you the cost first.

Where can I get help with Truss Analyzer?

Truss Analyzer is free at hmxtech.co.tz/truss. The Read Me explains how to model joints, members, supports and loads, and you can email us with questions.
