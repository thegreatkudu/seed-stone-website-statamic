Talk to our team
How long does it take to go live?

Most clinics are live in 30 days. We connect your EHR, import your schedule and train the front desk in two short sessions.

Which EHRs do you integrate with?

Cobalt, Summit, Ledgerline, Fernway and most FHIR or HL7 systems. Appointments, notes and claims sync both ways in real time.

Is Carefront HIPAA compliant?

Yes. Carefront is SOC 2 Type II audited and HITRUST certified, every plan includes a signed BAA, and data is encrypted at rest and in transit.

Can patients pay through Carefront?

Patients pay copays and balances from a text link or the patient app. Card on file and payment plans come standard on Practice and Network.

Do you charge per patient?

No. Pricing is per provider, per month. Front desk staff are free and there is no limit on patients or messages.

Can we bring our existing intake forms?

Send us your current PDFs and we rebuild them as mobile forms that write straight into the chart, usually within a week.
