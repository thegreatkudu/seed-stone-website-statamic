    title: 'Signing in'
    body: '<p>Go to <strong>/cp</strong> on this website and sign in with your staff account. Everything below is edited there and goes live as soon as you press <strong>Save</strong>. Ask the site administrator if you need an account or a password reset.</p>'
  -
    id: hxguide02
    title: 'Editing a page'
    body: '<p>Open <strong>Collections → Pages</strong> and pick the page. The tabs along the top follow the page from top to bottom: on the Home page, for example, <strong>Hero Slider</strong>, <strong>Features</strong>, <strong>Stages</strong> and so on down to <strong>Closing</strong>.</p><p>Lists such as questions, figures, cards and steps have an <strong>Add</strong> button; drag a row to move it, or remove it from its menu. Leaving an optional field empty hides that part of the page. Drawings and illustrations are part of the design and are not edited here.</p>'
  -
    id: hxguide03
    title: 'Menu and footer'
    body: '<p>Open <strong>Globals → Header</strong> for the menu links, the Truss Analyzer dropdown and the <strong>Register</strong> and <strong>Get a quote</strong> buttons. Open <strong>Globals → Footer</strong> for the tagline, the newsletter text, the link columns, the registrations and the legal links. Changes show on every page.</p><p>Links that start with / go to a page on this site. Links that start with https:// open in a new tab; tel: and mailto: links call or email.</p>'
  -
    id: hxguide04
    title: Photos
    body: '<p>Photos live under <strong>Assets</strong>. The Home hero slides and the Portfolio closing band each have a photo field. Use real, sharp project photos at least 2000 pixels wide, and fill in the image description so people using screen readers know what it shows. If a photo comes from someone else, add a credit on the <a href="/licenses">Licenses</a> page.</p>'
  -
    id: hxguide05
    title: Publications
    body: '<p>Open <strong>Collections → Publications</strong> and press <strong>Create entry</strong>. Add the title, topic and a one or two sentence summary, then upload the paper in <strong>Paper (PDF)</strong>. The file is stored on this website. Drag papers in the list to change their order. Turn on <strong>Featured</strong> to show a paper in the large card at the top of the page.</p>'
  -
    id: hxguide06
    title: 'News and articles'
    body: '<p>Open <strong>Collections → News</strong> and press <strong>Create entry</strong>, then choose <strong>News Item</strong> for events, training and announcements, or <strong>Article</strong> for a longer piece written by the team. Every entry gets its own page and appears on the News page, newest first by its date.</p><p>A news item has a type, a summary, the full story, optional event details (date, venue, organiser) and an optional attachment such as a poster or PDF, uploaded straight into the entry. An article can attach the paper it is based on.</p>'
  -
    id: hxguide07
    title: 'Quote requests and sign-ups'
    body: '<p>Every quote request is emailed to info@hmxtech.co.tz and saved under <strong>Forms → Project Enquiry</strong>. News sign-ups are saved under <strong>Forms → Newsletter</strong>. You can export both as a spreadsheet from the same screen. The thank-you and error messages are on each page’s own tabs.</p>'
  -
    id: hxguide08
    title: 'Company details and search'
    body: '<p>Open <strong>Globals → Settings</strong> to change the phone number, email, address, company description and the default text search engines show. Each page also has its own SEO title and description on its <strong>SEO</strong> tab.</p>'
  -
    id: hxguide09
    title: 'Legal pages'
    body: '<p>Privacy and Terms are made of sections, each a heading and its text. Have changes reviewed by a lawyer before saving, and update the “Last updated” date in the intro. The Licenses page lists the fonts, software, photos and tools the site uses, in groups.</p>'
  -
    id: hxguide10
    title: 'Colours, type and components'
    body: '<p>The brand colours, type and components are documented in the <a href="/style-guide">Style guide</a>. Changes to colours or layout are made by a developer in the website code, so the whole site stays consistent.</p>'
